Limits

How much, how fast

X counts follows and unfollows in one shared bucket: roughly 400 actions a day on a free account, 1,000 on Premium. Those are the ceilings where you get blocked, not targets. Accounts that stay under about 100–150 a day, spread out, almost never get flagged. These budgets carry across sessions and browser restarts.

Scoring

Moni score cleanup

Replaces the old Smart Cleanup. Uses the Smart Followers score that the Moni extension adds next to each account on X. The dedicated button on the popup unfollows everyone scoring below your threshold, including accounts that follow you back. Run Preview first to see who it would remove before anything happens. Requires the Moni extension: if it is missing, the popup will point you to install it.

Moni scores load a moment after each row appears, so the tool waits for them before acting and, if none show up, tells you Moni is missing. Get it at extension.getmoni.io.

Privacy

What this extension reports

So you know exactly what leaves your browser, here is the complete list. It is sent anonymously and is not tied to your X account.

  • A random ID generated on your device at install. It is not derived from you, your account or your hardware.
  • Extension version, browser, operating system and language, so the developer knows which builds to support.
  • An approximate location (country and city) worked out from your IP address on the server. Your IP address itself is never stored.
  • Run totals: which mode was used, how many accounts it unfollowed, how long it took, and whether X interrupted it.
 

Never sent: your X handle, any account you follow or unfollow, your archive, page contents, or your X password, which this extension never has. Everything in your archive stays on this device and is erased when you clear it or remove the extension.
